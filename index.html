LEARNING CENTRE

Blackjack foundations

Card values

2–10 count at face value. J, Q and K count as 10. An Ace counts as 1 or 11, whichever helps without busting.

How a round works

You and the dealer receive two cards. You act first: hit, stand, double or split when allowed. The dealer follows fixed table rules.

Hard vs soft totals

A hard 16 has no Ace counted as 11. A soft 16 includes an Ace counted as 11, so it can take a card without immediately busting.

Dealer upcard

The dealer's visible card matters. A 2–6 is often a weaker upcard; 7–Ace is stronger. Your best decision changes accordingly.

Basic strategy

A mathematically derived set of decisions based on your hand, dealer upcard and table rules. It can reduce the house edge, not guarantee a win.

House edge

The casino's average mathematical advantage over many rounds under specified rules and optimal play. Short sessions can vary widely.

Why betting systems don't beat the maths

Progressions such as Martingale increase bet size after losses. They do not change the expected value of each wager; table limits and a finite bankroll can stop the progression.

Variance means actual results fluctuate around long-run expectation. You can win a session despite a house edge, or lose despite making correct decisions.

Never gamble money you cannot afford to lose. This is education, not financial advice.
